MOMENTUM — Complaints and Athlete History

1. In Supabase SQL Editor, run momentum_support.sql once.
   Prerequisite: momentum_connections.sql already installed.
   History uses your existing momentum_tracking.sql and momentum_photos.sql.
2. Replace these files in the website root, keeping these exact names:
   support.html
   test10.html
   history.html
3. Deploy and reload the website.
4. Sign in as the athlete, open support.html and submit a complaint.
   An accepted trainer connection is required.
5. In a separate browser session, sign in as the trainer and open test10.html.
   Press Refresh, reply, and set Open / In Review / Resolved.
6. On the athlete page press Refresh messages to see the reply.
7. Open history.html. Choose dates or Subscription period, then Load period.
   Details come from saved daily activity; actual weights are separate from targets.
   Photos include dates even when no activity was logged.

Archive hides the case only for the current person; replies restore it.
Messages are in-app. Email notifications are not sent.
Previous local demo complaints and unscoped browser history are not imported.
Private photo links are temporary; open the photo again or reload to renew.

Validation: local PostgreSQL permission/RPC tests and DOM behavior tests passed.
Live Supabase and deployed browser verification must be completed after installation.
